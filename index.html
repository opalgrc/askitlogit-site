Ask It Log It

A calorie and nutrition tracker you talk to. Say what you ate, or ask what’s in something before you eat it. Your food log stays on your iPhone.

Help

Logging by voice

Tap Log it and say what you ate, like “two scrambled eggs and a slice of toast”. Understanding speech on the phone needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later). On other iPhones, the app can use a cloud lookup with your permission, and you can always add foods from the menu.

Restaurant meals and photos

Tap Check with Claude on the review card, or the camera button on Today. These are cloud lookups: 3 a day free, or 60 a day with Ask It Log It Pro. The app asks your permission before the first one.

Water

Tap + on the water card or on the widget for a glass, or say it with Log it: “a glass of water”. Tap the water card to add a bottle, take back a mistake, or change your daily goal.

Your data

Your log stays on your iPhone, and there are no accounts. Deleting the app deletes your log. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Ask It Log It Pro

  • Manage or cancel: in the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or use Manage subscription in the app’s Settings.
  • Restore after reinstalling, or on a new iPhone: in the app’s Settings, tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them at reportaproblem.apple.com.
